# Semantics @ Suren

**A structured learning journey into semantic retrieval, representation learning, and modern retrieval architectures.**

Semantics @ Suren documents a hands-on exploration of how machines represent meaning, how retrieval systems behave, and how their design choices influence relevance and performance.

The journey progresses through **Modules 0–8**, combining conceptual learning, controlled experiments, observations, and research interpretation.

## What This Repository Represents

This repository captures the learning outcomes: conceptual insights, experiment observations, and structured documentation.

The **Evidence Ledger** connects the learning to tangible evidence—recording what was explored, observed, and understood along the way.

The original working lab is maintained separately. The purpose here is to share the learning journey and its lessons, not to present a production-ready retrieval system.

## Learning Focus

- Semantic retrieval and retrieval diagnostics
- Semantic perturbation experiments
- Representation learning and embedding geometry
- Modern retrieval systems and ranking
- Alternative representations and dimensionality reduction
- Research interpretation and evidence-based conclusions

## The Guiding Principle

**Understand retrieval behavior before changing the system.**

The goal is not merely to build retrieval systems, but to develop the ability to reason about their behavior, evaluate their assumptions, and interpret evidence.
